        Table view

        How this is counted. Spending so far is the debit amount for rows dated October 2026 in the actual table (E6), through Oct 23. The plan is the debit amount on each T3 row (CJ6), the frozen start-of-month baseline. Balance checks, transfers, income and savings are left out.

        Still due comes from T2 (AT6), the updated plan: rows that still carry a placeholder date, by sub-category, minus what has already been paid on that sub-category. Rows ending in "_late" are matched to the bill they belong to. A line whose planned day has passed is tagged "past planned date".

        A line is unplanned when its sub-category has no T3 plan amount. Lines in T2 with no T3 amount, such as week-5 groceries and gas, show as still due but not as planned. "In the baseline, not expected now" lists T3 lines that T2 no longer carries and that have no payment. This is a draft: matching is by sub-category label, so a bill paid under a different label shows as both paid and still due.
